Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the warranty period for products? ▼
Most products come with a minimum 12-month manufacturer warranty. Some premium items like laptops and smartphones offer up to 24 months. Extended warranty options are available at checkout.
Do you offer international shipping? ▼
Yes, we currently ship to most countries in Europe and selected countries worldwide. Shipping fees and delivery times vary by location and will be shown at checkout.
How long does delivery take? ▼
Standard delivery within the UK takes 2–5 business days. Express delivery is available for next-day or 48-hour service in most areas.
Can I return or exchange a product? ▼
Yes, you have 30 days from the date of delivery to return unused products in original packaging for a full refund or exchange. Please contact our customer service team to initiate a return.
Are the prices inclusive of VAT? ▼
All prices displayed on our website include VAT. No additional tax will be added at checkout.
Do you sell genuine brand products? ▼
Absolutely. All products sold on TechGizmoHub are 100% genuine and sourced directly from authorized distributors or manufacturers.
How can I track my order? ▼
Once your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking number via email. You can track your package using the link provided or by logging into your account.
Do you offer price matching? ▼
Yes, we offer price matching on identical products from major UK retailers. Contact our customer service team with the competitor’s price and product link for verification.
